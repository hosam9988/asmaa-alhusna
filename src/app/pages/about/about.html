About the book

Fiqh al-Asma’ al-Husna (“Understanding the Beautiful Names”) is by Shaykh ‘Abd ar-Razzaq ibn ‘Abd al-Muhsin al-‘Abbad al-Badr, a scholar of Madinah. It is written in short chapters. It opens with chapters on the worth of knowing Allah’s names and attributes and on the principles of this subject, then explains the Beautiful Names one by one: what each name means and points to, and what it does to a servant’s heart, worship and supplication, with evidence from the Qur’an, the Sunnah and the words of the scholars.

The text on this site is from the book’s second edition (Madinah, 1430 AH), as published on the Shaykh’s official website, with its page numbers given for every chapter. The 1st edition (Dar al-Tawhid, Riyadh, 1429 AH) is paged differently and has a few small differences; for example, the name ar-Raziq was added in the 2nd edition.

The categories of Tawheed

Tawheed means singling out Allah alone. Scholars explain it in three categories:

Lordship (Rububiyyah)

Believing that Allah alone creates, owns and controls all things.

Worship (Uluhiyyah)

Directing all worship to Allah alone: we pray to, hope in and worship none but Him.

Names & Attributes (al-Asma’ wa as-Sifat)

Affirming the names and attributes that Allah and His Messenger ﷺ affirmed for Him, in the way that befits His majesty.

﴿ لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ ۖ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ ﴾

“There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.”

Ash-Shura 42:11
