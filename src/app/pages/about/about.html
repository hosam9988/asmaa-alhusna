About the book

Tafsir Asma' Allah al-Husna (“Explanation of the Beautiful Names of Allah”) gathers what Shaykh Abdur-Rahman ibn Nasir as-Sa‘di wrote about the Names of Allah across his tafsir and other books. It was compiled by Shaykh ‘Ubayd ibn ‘Ali al-‘Ubayd, who arranged the names in Arabic alphabetical order (Islamic University of Madinah, 1421 AH). For each name it explains what it means, which attributes of perfection it points to, and how it should shape a believer’s heart and actions.

This site presents the names in the same order as the book, with the page number of the printed edition.

Shaykh as-Sa‘di (1307–1376 AH) was a scholar from Unaizah in al-Qassim, author of the famous tafsir Taysir al-Karim ar-Rahman. Among his best-known students was Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-‘Uthaymin. May Allah have mercy on them both.

The categories of Tawheed

Tawheed means singling out Allah alone. Scholars explain it in three categories:

Lordship (Rububiyyah)

Believing that Allah alone creates, owns and controls all things.

Worship (Uluhiyyah)

Directing all worship to Allah alone: we pray to, hope in and worship none but Him.

Names & Attributes (al-Asma’ wa as-Sifat)

Affirming the names and attributes that Allah and His Messenger ﷺ affirmed for Him, in the way that befits His majesty.

Principles about Allah’s names

  • We only call Allah by the names He gave Himself or that His Messenger ﷺ used; we do not invent names for Him.
  • Every one of His names is beautiful and points to an attribute of perfection.
  • We affirm them as they came, without distorting or denying their meanings, and without asking “how” or likening Allah to His creation.
  • “Preserving” the names means learning them, understanding their meanings, calling on Allah by them and worshipping Him according to what they teach.

“There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.”
